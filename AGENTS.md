# Working on Bento

- Prioritize quality, simplicity, robustness, scalability, and long-term maintainability over development cost when making technical decisions.
- Keep commits small, narrow, and self-contained. Flag unrelated changes for follow-up commits rather than mixing them into the current commit.
- Prefer small, self-contained methods over larger, multipurpose methods.
- Take pride in your work, enjoy the process, and let's build great things together.
